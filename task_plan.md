## Constraints

- Do not rewrite the six-artifact MinerU contract or promote `cleaned.md` back into the indexing input path.
- Do not break the P2/P3 citation DTOs or the current `RetrievalResult` / `RetrievalResponse` boundary.
- Unification means one final chunk policy, not one raw parser/splitter for every file type.
- Dense embedding may become heading-aware, but user-facing `content` should stay as display-ready original text.
- Doc-level dedup is not a substitute for parent-child chunking; they solve different problems.
